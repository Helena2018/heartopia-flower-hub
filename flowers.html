Pansy (三色堇)

Starter Flower
🌸

Base Colors (Seeds): Red, Yellow, White

Hybrid Formulas:

  • Orange: Red + Yellow (~50%)
  • Blue: White + White (~25%)
  • Purple: Blue + Blue OR Red + Blue (~15%)

Rose (玫瑰)

Legendary / Rare
🌹

Base Colors (Seeds): Red, Yellow, White

Hybrid Formulas:

  • Pink: Red + White (50%)
  • Black: Red + Red (25%)
  • Blue Rose: Hybrid Red + Hybrid Red (~10%)
  • Gold Rose: Black + Black watered with Gold Water Can
