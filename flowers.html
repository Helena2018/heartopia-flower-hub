💡 Heartopia Breeding Key Tips & Rules (心动小镇育种核心规则):
  • Cross Pattern: Plant parent flowers adjacent (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) with open soil next to them.
  • Gold Watering Can: Required for breeding Gold Roses from Black Roses.
  • Visitor Bonus: Friends watering your flower beds increases hybrid mutation rate for the next day.

Pansy (三色堇)

Starter Flower
🌸

Base Colors (Seeds): Red, Yellow, White

Hybrid Formulas:

  • Orange: Red + Yellow (~50%)
  • Blue: White + White (~25%)
  • Purple: Blue + Blue OR Red + Blue (~15%)

Rose (玫瑰)

Legendary / Rare
🌹

Base Colors (Seeds): Red, Yellow, White

Hybrid Formulas:

  • Pink: Red + White (50%)
  • Black: Red + Red (25%)
  • Blue Rose: Hybrid Red + Hybrid Red (Rare ~10%)
  • Gold Rose: Black + Black watered with Gold Water Can
